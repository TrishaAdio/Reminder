# RemindAni design brief (v2)

1. **Feel.** *Calm*: urgency comes from placement, dimming and sound, never from red, shaking or exclamation marks. *Precise*: every size, gap, radius and curve is a token. *Warm*: warm neutrals, one amber accent for actions, muted tints for identity. No glass, no gradients.
2. **Hierarchy.** Each screen has one large element: Today's countdown (40px) inside a live ring, the editor's 28px title, the popup's 32px message. Everything else stays at 15/13.
3. **Type.** Inter Variable, bundled. Six sizes: 40 / 28 / 22 / 15 / 13 / 11 (the reminder card, read from across the desk, steps up to 32 / 16 / 14), weights 400/500/600, sentence case, tabular figures for times. Segoe UI only as fallback.
4. **Color.** Neutral layers window → card → raised, each a tonal step, lit by a 1px top highlight in dark mode. Amber (#A84F0A light, #F0A040 dark) is used only for actions and selection. Six preset tints share one oklch lightness and chroma and differ only in hue: indigo 278, blue 240, green 155, orange 52, rose 12, sand 82. Tints mark identity (tiles, ring, timeline), never actions. All text pairs are 4.5:1 or better (checked).
5. **Space and shape.** 4px grid. Concentric radii: popup 36 = buttons 20 + 16 inset = tile 12 + 24 inset; list card 20 = rows 16 + 4 inset; control 10 = thumb 8 + 2 inset. One shadow recipe in two strengths (resting and lifted); the lifted one is pre-rendered and only fades.
6. **Motion.** Springs only, solved in JS and emitted as CSS `linear()`. Enter k260 c24 (ζ .74, within 1% at 408ms) · Exit k700 c52 (243ms) · Press k3000 c110 · Release k520 c26 · Layout k320 c30 · Toggle k600 c34.
   Tabs cross-fade, pushed pages slide 32px, and the tab thumb, segmented thumbs, toggles and position preview glide. Rows reorder and leave with FLIP. The toggle knob stretches while pressed, buttons squash to 0.97, and the check wipes in on Done. The popup drops 16px and scales up from 0.96 over a fading dim; Wait shrinks it toward the tray, Done settles it in place.
   Done is a full-screen reward (`popup/celebrate.js`). The card jumps, a shockwave and flash leave the button, and a praise badge pops up with today's tally and streak. One of eleven effects then fills the screen, each with its own pieces, synthesized sound and picture reaction: confetti cannons, fireworks, hearts, star shower, bubbles, blossom storm, balloons, butterflies, rainbow, sparkle swirl and paw prints. They come from a shuffled bag, so all eleven play before any repeats. Consecutive Done answers add up to 90% more pieces and raise the chime up to five semitones. Pieces are drawn on one canvas by `popup/particles.js` at the display's own refresh rate, with physics integrated from real frame time in 1/480 s substeps and shapes cached as sprites. Fast pieces stretch along their path. Reduced motion and the solid card keep only the ripple, badge and sound.
   Transform and opacity only (springs are sampled at 240 points a second for high-refresh screens). Exceptions: the celebration canvas, and, done once, the ring's arc length (paint on a 136px SVG). Reduced motion swaps movement for 150ms cross-fades and stops idle loops.
7. **Glass look (opt-in, Settings › General › Style).** It's the one exception to "no glass, no gradients", in an iOS style. It uses iOS label and fill colours, system blue (#007AFF / #0A84FF) for actions, and green switches (51 × 31). Buttons, segmented controls and the tab bar are capsules, titles are 34px bold, and list headers are small caps. Surfaces are frosted: a fine grain, a sheen from the lit top edge, a cool glow in the lower corner, a light-catching gradient bevel (bright top-left, faint bottom-right) and `blur(30px) saturate(2.1)`. Glass only reads as glass when there is something to frost, so the wallpaper has distinct colour blobs that the cards sit across. On Windows 11 22H2+ the window is also acrylic and the blobs are softer, so the desktop shows between them. Tokens live in `tokens.css` (`:root.glass`) and component rules in `main/glass.css`. The reminder card frosts what is in its own window (the dim, the picture's lower edge, waiting cards).
8. **Fallbacks.** Without transparency or GPU compositing, the popup becomes a square, solid card with a hairline, at the chosen position, with no dim.
